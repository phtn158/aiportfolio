Kskinluxe — Website

The consumer-facing site for Kskinluxe, an AI-powered skincare brand — built end-to-end as the proof-of-concept client for Thussisters&Co's AI-first digital services model.

Tools: Claude · No-code build

📄 Spec 📝 Build notes 🔗 Live site

The Problem

Kskinluxe is a brand-new business with no digital footprint. As a sole business owner with a technical background, I needed a website — but wanted to build it cost-effectively rather than commit to a traditional engineering spend before knowing whether there was real demand. The site also needed to double as the landing page for social media marketing and ads, to measure interest and intent before launching the business physically.

My Role & Approach

My role

Product owner, designer, developer, QA, and domain expert — all one person. No handoffs, no team; every decision from scope to shipping was mine to make and execute.

My approach

Used Claude to build the site — the simplest and cheapest way to get something real in front of people. The goal wasn't a polished final product; it was testing the water before spending more than needed to validate demand.

What It Does

  • Creates a foundation for building business awareness
  • Informs visitors of the services Kskinluxe will offer
  • Includes an embedded waitlist to measure potential demand ahead of launch

Outcome

The website is live, but the business hasn't launched physically yet — so there's no demand or conversion data available at this stage.

  • Admin / user profile — account and order management for returning customers

AI Skin Analysis

A photo-based feature embedded in the Kskinluxe site that analyzes a user's skin and generates personalized product recommendations.

Tools: Claude · Claude API · No-code build

📄 Spec 📝 Build notes 🔗 Live site

The Problem

Skincare shoppers buying online have no equivalent to the in-person consultation standard in Korea, where every salon visit starts with an assessment of what a client's skin actually needs. Without that, they're left guessing — buying based on marketing or trends rather than what's right for their skin, which drives returns and erodes trust on a first purchase. I wanted to bring that consultation experience to Kskinluxe.

My Role & Approach

My role

Product owner, designer, developer, QA, and domain expert — all one person. No handoffs, no team; every decision from scope to shipping was mine to make and execute.

My approach

Chose to build the feature myself rather than purchase an in-salon AI skin analysis device — the devices Korean salons use are expensive and built for in-person use, not a web product. Built the feature using Claude — no custom-trained model, just direct Claude API calls. Made a deliberate tradeoff of two API calls per request instead of one, prioritizing response quality and speed over API cost, since this is often a visitor's first impression of the brand and building trust for a first-time visit mattered more than shaving cost.

What It Does

  • User uploads 1–3 photos of their skin
  • Identifies skin type and key concerns
  • Recommends ingredients and treatments suited to their skin
  • Suggests an at-home maintenance routine
  • Suggests weekly clinic treatments

Outcome

The website is live, but the business hasn't launched physically yet — so there's no demand or conversion data available at this stage.

What's Next

  • Measure accuracy — evaluate how well the recommendations actually match dermatological guidance before scaling usage
  • Consider building a proprietary model — evaluate whether a purpose-built model outperforms relying solely on the Claude API
  • Add a cost-control barrier — likely user login, to prevent unlimited free usage from driving up API costs
  • Address privacy — define how uploaded photos are handled, stored, and protected
  • Add profile storage — let users save their photos and track skin treatment progress over time
